# Suggested corrections for manual manuscript revision

These notes are separate from the original PDF. No manuscript was edited or regenerated.

- **Abstract (page 1), Results (pages 16–17), Discussion/Conclusion (pages 19–21):** Replace claims that original LightSAKT uniformly outperforms all baselines. DKT has higher mean test AUC on ASSIST2009; DKT+ and SAKT have higher mean AUC on Algebra2005. The item-augmented model is a distinct information comparison. Do not attribute its gain solely to linear attention.
- **Tables 4–7 and Figures 4–6 (pages 14–17):** The manuscript's original LightSAKT AUCs 0.8658, 0.9175 and 0.8923 and associated curves are not reproduced under the corrected causal protocol. Replace them with verified values and document the protocol/provenance change. Do not combine old and new results or describe the difference as an effect of adding difficulty. Historical causal-correction evidence remains in the earlier research reports.
- **Table 2 and preprocessing (pages 8–9):** Replace unsupported processed counts, including Algebra's 3,967 records, with actual counts and explicitly distinguish raw release/subset rows from retained interactions. The pipeline uses one-overlap windows and retains every per-student n−1 target; it does not truncate each student to only 100 observations. Specify student-disjoint 70/10/20 splitting, training-only vocabulary/UNK, composite skills and independent item identity.
- **Table 3 (page 13):** Uniform learning rate 0.005, dimensions, heads, dropout and sequence lengths do not describe the actual selected configurations. Use model-specific settings, including Algebra length 200 and native DKT/DKT+ logit dropout. Report Adam settings, five seeds, epoch ceiling, patience, actual epochs and selection criterion. EdNet settings were prespecified, not selected by a fabricated grid.
- **Architecture (pages 9–11):** The implemented q indexes skills or composite skills, not independently identified items. The model has one causal ELU+1 encoder. Position embeddings apply to past interaction embeddings, not question queries. The residual adds past interaction and query embeddings to attention output. The FFN is d→d/2→d, rather than d→d→d. Difficulty leaves this structure unchanged and adds one learned scalar times a fitted item-versus-skill logit correction. Fitted rates add information/capacity beyond the scalar.
- **Figure 7 and efficiency text (pages 17–18, 20):** Replace the fixed-num_q=100 illustration with actual dataset-specific checkpoint counts. Parameter counts alone do not establish matched training time, memory use or scalability. Dimensions, vocabularies, hardware, pacing and search exposure differ. Fitted difficulty statistics also have storage and computation costs.
- **Wilcoxon analysis (page 18):** Do not reuse W=78/p=0.00048 without its independent observations. New exact paired n=5 tests with Holm correction do not reject at 0.05. Report conditional fixed-split/selection uncertainty. Configurations and epochs are not independent repetitions.
- **Table 8 and error analysis (pages 18, 20):** Component removal does not uniformly hurt: position removal and single-head attention can improve Algebra AUC. No corrected layer-normalization-removal or EdNet component ablation was completed. The no_regularization label aliases no_dropout and is not independent evidence. Adding item information is a separate comparison from component removal.
- **ASSIST2015 statements (pages 1, 8, 12, 15–16, 21):** Replace the primary third dataset with the exact EdNet-KT1 5,000-student first-question-per-session subset. Keep ASSIST2015 evidence historical; no valid difficulty comparison exists without independent item IDs. Never relabel an old ASSIST2015 curve as EdNet.
- **Figures 8–9 and mastery/deployment claims (pages 19–21):** Next-response probabilities do not establish validated mastery, latent clusters or monotonic knowledge acquisition. Use the new descriptive examples only with a new title/caption, or omit unverified figures. Offline fitted statistics can use future observations of other training students, so real-time/chronological validity is not demonstrated. Feature-matched SAKT + Difficulty, independent cohorts and chronological evaluation remain needed.
- **External leaderboard comparisons:** AKT and DTransformer were not retrained under this protocol. Cross-paper numbers with different splits, preprocessing, information and settings cannot establish superiority. Retain PENDING groups and describe the package as verified interim evidence, not publication-ready completion of the nine-model campaign.
