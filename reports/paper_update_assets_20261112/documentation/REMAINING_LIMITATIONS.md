# Remaining limitations and exact blockers

**Resources:** The visible historical shared MIG has technical headroom, but current allocation ownership and a new total execution budget are unconfirmed. The previous 15-run scope is consumed. No GPU training was launched. Retain the existing 4 GiB allocator, 900-second windows, admission/runtime memory floors, host-RAM cap, pacing and step-time safeguards if a new allocation is granted. An idle device is not permission. At least 58 selected-configuration training outcomes remain, plus validation-only Algebra2005 GKT selection. A 58×900-second one-window planning envelope is 52,200 seconds (14.5 hours), not an approved budget or a guarantee that models fit. EdNet GKT needs authorized memory/runtime profiling first; extra resume windows and search trials need explicit accounting.

**Coverage:** Fifteen of 27 groups have all five seeds. ASSIST2009 GKT and GKT-MHA each have one newly evaluated selected seed-42 checkpoint, with four other seeds missing. Algebra2005 GKT settings still require selection. EdNet DKT, DKT+, DKVMN, GKT and GKT-MHA require training. SAKT + Difficulty requires training on all three datasets. The 72 completed ASSIST2009 GKT candidates are not independent seeds. Old primary runs do not silently replace selected-final protocols. No partial mean or single-seed score fills a five-seed aggregate. Historical ASSIST2015 status markers are not current jobs or current primary comparisons.

**Information and implementation:** Difficulty provides item information absent from skill-only models. SAKT + Difficulty is the necessary feature-matched control and is still untrained. The original LightSAKT architecture itself does not beat every available baseline. Repository GKT variants omit canonical erase-add gating and MLP batch normalization. Search exposure is unequal; GKT batch size 8 makes more updates than batch 64 at the same epoch ceiling. Different devices and pacing prevent matched timing or memory-efficiency claims. External fitted statistics add estimator capacity not reflected by one extra trainable scalar.

**Leakage and time:** Repeated actual-data perturbations and frozen-sidecar reconstruction found no held-out-label or own-student self-label leakage under the offline protocol. Training features exclude the whole same student. Held-out features use training labels only; item smoothing is 10, skill prior Beta(1,1), unseen item correction zero. Later observations of OTHER training students remain available to the offline aggregates. The EdNet audit flags later training information for 99.6012% of validation and 99.5887% of test targets. Forward-time deployment performance has not been established.

**Population and preprocessing:** ASSIST2009 item identity is authentic problem_id; Algebra uses a problem/step composite and a local split of the original training subset; EdNet uses authentic question_id and full composite tag sets. EdNet is exactly the existing uniform 5,000-student first-question-per-session subset, selected before outcomes with seed 20261104, not full KT1. Its first question is chosen before answer filtering. Dataset bytes, split hashes and masks are unchanged. Item coverage differs: Algebra has about 26.29% unseen test items, EdNet about 0.165%. Long histories dominate pooled metrics; the largest EdNet validation student contributes 19.11% of targets. These differences affect interpretation across datasets.

**Statistics:** Five initialization seeds share one split per dataset; SD is optimization variability, not independent cohort uncertainty. Some seed-42 checkpoints were reused from validation-only searches, adding selection conditioning. Nine exact two-sided paired Wilcoxon tests with Holm correction do not reject at 0.05. With five nonzero pairs, minimum two-sided exact p is 0.0625. The old manuscript W=78/p=0.00048 claim is not reproduced, and epochs/configurations cannot be treated as repetitions. Independent cohorts/splits and chronological studies remain needed. Test results are reporting-only and must not guide future tuning.

**Prediction origin:** Thirty older saved arrays contain original GPU probabilities, whereas the earlier master table used CPU replay metrics. The maximum checked metric difference is 1.85e-8; every exact difference is recorded. This package consistently recalculates float64 test metrics from the referenced genuine files without overwriting history. Validation AUC remains the native checkpoint-selection score. Threshold is 0.5; MSE is probability error; BCE clips to [1e-7, 1−1e-7].

**Visualizations:** Student probability traces are genuine saved predictions, not mastery or validated latent representations. Items/skills can vary and context windows reset. No certified replacement for the original latent-space/mastery figures was found. EdNet component ablations and layer-normalization removals remain unavailable. No AI graph generation, random outcomes, curve interpolation, missing-value imputation or invented confidence intervals was used.
