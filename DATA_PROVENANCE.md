# Data provenance and availability

Raw sources missing from the shared Drive folder were copied read-only from existing local material into `data/raw/`. Checksums identify exactly what was executed. Historical pickle preprocessing was never trusted or consumed. The copied local raw files' byte identity with current publisher downloads has not been independently established; these are documented local variants rather than falsely claiming new publisher downloads.

* ASSIST2009: `/workspace/LightSAKT_Project/learning#/train/datasets/ASSIST2009/skill_builder_data.csv`, 525,534 rows. Publisher: https://sites.google.com/site/assistmentsdata/home/2009-2010-assistment-data/skill-builder-data-2009-2010 . Publisher distinguishes a duplicated legacy release from a corrected collapsed-tag release. Our raw file has separate tags; the cleaning rule collapses an answered attempt's tags. This is a reproducible new cleaning protocol, not a claim of byte identity with the publisher's corrected release.
* ASSIST2015: existing locally recovered four-column publisher release, `2015_100_skill_builders_main_problems.csv`, SHA256 `75e1f46131d5897a388c82411bc3032279899cb1f1e66a9043354d41234407a0`. Local historical acquisition evidence names publisher file ID `0B_hO8cnpcIMgUGZzRnh3bHJrSjQ`. Publisher page https://sites.google.com/site/assistmentsdata/datasets/2015-assistments-skill-builder-data was checked in this rebuild and identifies the top 100 skill builders. Raw 708,631 records include nonbinary labels; 683,801 remain under the fixed binary filter.
* Algebra: `/workspace/LightSAKT_Project/learning#/train/datasets/algebra_2005_2006/algebra_2005_2006_train.txt`, 809,694 rows. Publisher https://www.kdd.org/kdd-cup/view/kdd-cup-2010-student-performance-evaluation/Data distinguishes full development records, training and test subsets. Our available training file is the 19-column subset and is re-split by student; official test/master prediction files in the Drive snapshot are not concatenated without a verified row join. No claim of full official 813,661-step coverage is made.

Publisher pages were reviewed on 2026-10-13. Public research access is documented, but no permissive redistribution license was established. KDD rules describe registered-participant downloads. Raw data, student-level predictions, and checkpoints are omitted from ordinary Git history; they are retained in the workspace archive with checksum manifests. Treat downstream redistribution according to publisher terms. Exact counts, missing-field exclusions, identifiers, overlap checks and raw/processed/split hashes are in `reports/<dataset>_statistics.json`.
